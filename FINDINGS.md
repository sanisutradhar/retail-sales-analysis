# Findings — Synthetic Retail Demonstration

> **Important:** This is fictional, programmatically generated data. These findings do not describe a real retailer and must not be presented as real business evidence.

- The dataset contains **1,669 transaction lines**, **1,270 orders**, and **180 fictional customers**.
- **179 customers (99.4%)** placed at least two orders in the generated year.
- Average monthly revenue in November–December was **31.1%** higher than the January–October monthly average by design.
- Highest-revenue generated product: **Water Bottle** (5,400.00 fictional currency units).

## Interpretation and limitations

The seasonal uplift and product/customer patterns are artifacts of the generation rules, not discoveries from observed customer behavior. Use this dataset to demonstrate code and chart formatting only. For a job portfolio, replace it with a public real-world dataset and rerun the analysis before making business recommendations.
